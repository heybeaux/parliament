Methodology & assumptions

The calculator computes an upper-bound annual rework cost for the single-shot LLM path and compares it to the Parliament path's rework cost plus deliberation cost. The math:

  • decisionsPerYear = decisionsPerMonth × 12
  • badSingle = decisionsPerYear × (singleShotErrorRate / 100)
  • badParliament = decisionsPerYear × (parliamentErrorRate / 100)
  • reworkCostSingle = badSingle × reworkHours × rate
  • reworkCostParliament = badParliament × reworkHours × rate
  • deliberationSpend = decisionsPerYear × parliamentCost
  • savings = reworkCostSingle − (reworkCostParliament + deliberationSpend)

Caveats:

  • Rework hours is a single-point estimate. Real rework distributions are long-tailed; a single bad architectural decision can cost weeks. The calculator is intentionally conservative.
  • The error-rate inputs are estimates. Parliament publishes a benchmark corpus (parliament.app/benchmark) with measured residue and resolution rates per question category — recalibrate the inputs against your domain.
  • Parliament doesn't replace human judgement. The wrong-confidence rate measures how often Parliament masks uncertainty, not how often it produces the right answer. Honest "we don't know" is the load-bearing distinction.
  • This calculator is open-source. Source at github.com/heybeaux/parliament.
